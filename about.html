About OM GRAPHICS

30+ Years of dedicated printing excellence, serving Indian and Multinational brands since 1996.

Mr. Uttam Nimkar

Director & Founder

From Director's Desk

OM GRAPHICS is one of the leading commercial printers based in Mumbai, offering high-quality sheet-fed print jobs for a wide range of products & brands. Established in 1996, it has been associated with some of India's most well-known companies, MNCs, and advertising agencies.

Today, OM GRAPHICS is the preferred printer for anyone seeking excellence in print. OM GRAPHICS deploys world-class technology and stringent quality control at every stage of the print assignment. In addition, it works closely with its clients and creative community, delivering a great degree of versatility and customization in the execution of every job, across a spectrum of different materials. Over the years, OM GRAPHICS has pioneered several new and innovative print techniques.

We have recently expanded our capacities to cater to ever-increasing customer requirements. Our dynamic and dedicated group of people is willing to help you with each and every print requirement. Consider this brochure as an invitation to come and visit our facilities and experience our print.
