Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for print jobs? +
MOQs vary by technique. For offset printing (Komori Lithrone), typical MOQs start at 1,000 units for brochures and 500 for cartons. For digital printing on our Xerox setup, we accept runs as low as 100 units.
Where is your printing plant located? +
Our integrated corporate office and production facility are in Mumbai — convenient for dispatch across Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, and Thane.
Are you audited for corporate B2B compliance? +
Yes. As a vendor for MNCs like Johnson & Johnson, Pfizer, and Asian Paints, our plant is audited for safety, environment controls, and maintains a dust-free packaging area compliant with corporate B2B requirements.
