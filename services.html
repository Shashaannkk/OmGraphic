What We Print

Our Printing Capabilities

We offer comprehensive print solutions spanning commercial catalogs, pharmaceutical cartons, and visual marketing assets — all produced in our Mumbai plant.

Publications & Marketing

Books

Hardcover and paperback corporate journals, manuals, booklets, and directories stitched or perfect-bound in-house.

Brochures

Multipage double-sided marketing catalogs, saddle-stitched or spiral bound with spot UV gloss options.

Catalogues

Fashion catalogs and product guides utilizing heavy art paper with clean section partitions.

Leaflets

Precision folded corporate flyers, marketing leaflets, and detailed pharmaceutical inserts.

Shade Cards

Accurate color match cards showing cosmetic swatches, dye gradients, and paint shades.

Visual Aids

Sturdy medical detail books, spiral-bound easel charts, and desk manuals for field representatives.

Packaging & Cartons

Cartons

Mono cartons, cosmetic boxes, and drug boxes printed on SBS board with custom die cuts.

Blister Cards

Heat-seal backing cards for commercial consumer electronics and pharmaceutical pill packs.

Self-Adhesive Labels

Sticky product labels, barcode labels, and product decals printed in roll or sheet configurations.

Dispensers

Sturdy retail counter cardboard boxes holding individual products, die-cut for easy dispensing.

Gimmick Labels

Specialized visual labels containing pop-up structures or custom fold elements for product branding.

Corporate & Event Collaterals

Calendars

Corporate desk tent calendars and wall calendars customized with metal wiro bindings.

Direct Mailers

Self-mailing catalogs, corporate brochures, and folders styled for direct courier distribution.

Stationery

High-grade letterheads, customized envelopes, and business cards matching corporate brand identities.

Invitation Cards

Custom-cut corporate event invites, wedding invitations, and luxury envelopes with foil details.

Pouch Folders

Gusseted file folders and custom paper pocket sheets to securely hold A4 document kits.

Point of Sale & Custom Fabrications

Danglers

Double-sided retail danglers with pre-punched holes for display cords, die-cut to round shapes.

Fabricated Jobs

Specialized manual folding box designs, rigid box casings, and custom cardboard mounts.

Collateral Kits

Corporate onboarding boxes, product launch binders, and structured marketing presentation kits.

Spiral / Wiro Jobs

Twin loop metal wire bindings for catalogs, reference handbooks, and notebooks.
